Overview

Built from
the
inside

As co-founder and creative director, every frame, every asset, and every story is created with care and attention.

GZO Experience is a cross-cultural brand bringing Japanese-inspired clothing and cultural experiences to a global audience. Building it meant working across every discipline at once — developing the visual identity, directing and producing content, and building the digital infrastructure that holds it all together.

The work has been ongoing since inception: a continuous cycle of creative strategy, content production, and platform development as the brand has evolved from concept into a functioning cross-cultural enterprise.

This project is a living body of work — and the proof that effective brand building requires more than good content. It requires genuine cultural fluency.

Web & E-commerce

Designed
& built

The brand needed a platform that matched the ambition of the content — so we built it. The GZO Experience website and e-commerce platform was designed and developed in-house, from architecture through to launch and ongoing maintenance.

The build covers brand site, product store, and bilingual content — a full-stack creative responsibility that sits at the intersection of design, development, and cultural positioning.
